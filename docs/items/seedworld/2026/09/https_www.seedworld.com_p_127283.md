# Corn Genetic Diversity Warning Issued by Researchers

A new study warns that corn genetic diversity is critically low for grain yield in intermediate-maturity stiff-stalk lines. Researchers say the finding could limit breeders’ ability to develop high-yielding hybrids for changing growing conditions.
