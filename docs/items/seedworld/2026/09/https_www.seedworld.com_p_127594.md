# Albaugh Seed Treatments: Right from the Start.

When Iowa farmer Dennis Albaugh founded the company in 1979, it was more than a business venture. It was a response to a challenge he knew firsthand. He believed farmers deserved greater access to affordable, high-quality crop protection solutions. With that vision, he mortgaged his home, purchased a used truck and a supply of crop protection products, and began serving neighboring farmers throughout Iowa. From those humble beginnings, Albaugh was built by a farmer, for farmers. More than four decades later, while the company has grown into a global enterprise, the principles that inspired its founding remain unchanged. We continue to believe that farmers deserve reliable, cost-effective solutions that help them succeed from planting through harvest. Today, Albaugh remains family-owned and headquartered in Ankeny, Iowa, proudly rooted in the community where it all began. As the world’s largest privately held supplier of crop protection products, Albaugh delivers a growing portfolio of post-patent and innovative crop protection solutions, seed treatments, and turf and ornamental products. Our independence allows us to stay focused on what matters most: helping farmers succeed while delivering value, reliability, and service through our distributor partners. That commitment is supported by our manufacturing and packaging facility in St. Joseph, Missouri, located in the heart of the Midwest and within one day’s transit of 65% of U.S. corn and soybean acres. This strategic location provides significant logistical advantages and helps ensure efficient, dependable delivery to customers across the country. At the heart of Albaugh’s success is the same promise Dennis made on day one: high-quality products, exceptional service, and competitive pricing. It’s how we continue to unlock the power of choice for our customers and honor our roots every day. That same commitment to value and performance is reflected throughout Albaugh’s seed treatment portfolio. Albaugh offers alternatives to branded products across more than 80 active ingredients and 200 formulations in the North American market. Our seed treatment solutions give customers the flexibility to create differentiated offerings, from single-active products to customized blends tailored to specific agronomic needs. The portfolio includes trusted traditional chemistries such as Ace® 3.8L ST (ipconazole), Anchor™ 3L ST (metalaxyl-M), Legend™ 5L ST (thiamethoxam), and our newest additions, Insectiprole™ 5L ST (chlorantraniliprole) and Prothioconazole 4L ST, alongside the BIOST® Nature’s Science™ line of biological seed treatment solutions. Each product is designed and field-tested to deliver excellent handling characteristics, formulation stability, seed safety, and consistent performance. Albaugh remains committed to helping farmers make the most of every acre. Our seed treatment portfolio reflects that farmer-founded approach, delivering dependable performance, trusted quality, and competitive value. Right for the seed. Right from the start.
