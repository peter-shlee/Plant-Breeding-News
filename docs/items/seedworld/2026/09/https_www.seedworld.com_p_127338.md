# Embrapa Outlines Measures for Crop Climate Risks

Embrapa has outlined 163 measures to help Brazilian crop producers manage climate risks including drought, excessive rainfall, heat, storms, frost and fire. The recommendations were developed in response to El Niño conditions expected during the 2026-27 growing season.
