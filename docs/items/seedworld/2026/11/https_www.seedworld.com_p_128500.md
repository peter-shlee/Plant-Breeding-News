# Unlocking Wild Plant Traits Could Help Breed More Resilient Crops

New chromosome recognition research at Aberystwyth University aims to help plant breeders access valuable traits from wild relatives. The three-year project will combine artificial intelligence, DNA sequencing and chromosome painting to investigate barriers to conventional breeding and identify opportunities to develop more resilient crops.
