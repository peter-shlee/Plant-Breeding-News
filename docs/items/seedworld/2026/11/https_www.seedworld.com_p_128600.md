# Matching Crop Choice to Tree Age Could Protect Agroforestry Yields

New research from the University of Göttingen suggests alley cropping systems can maintain cereal yields when farmers match crop rotations to tree growth. Winter crops performed similarly to those in treeless fields, while spring crops experienced yield reductions as poplar trees matured, highlighting opportunities to improve agroforestry management.
