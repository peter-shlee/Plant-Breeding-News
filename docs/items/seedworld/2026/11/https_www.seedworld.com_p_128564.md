# Plant Immune Memory Offers New Potential for Crop Breeding

Researchers at the University of Sheffield have uncovered how plant immune memory can persist while protecting genome stability. The findings, alongside early results in rice, point to potential applications in epigenetic crop breeding, offering new ways to develop disease resistance, stress tolerance and other valuable traits.
