# WheatAI Could Speed Up Breeding and Help Farmers Assess Wheat Crops

WheatAI, a web-based tool under development at South Dakota State University, uses artificial intelligence to automate wheat trait measurements from field images. Researchers hope the technology will help breeders screen more wheat lines, assess disease and give farmers additional information for estimating yield potential and making harvest decisions.
