# How a Wild Wheat from Iraq Helped Breed Three Durum Varieties

Wild wheat collected in Iraq in 1978 has contributed to three durum wheat varieties developed for farmers in Morocco, Syria and Lebanon. The breeding effort highlights how genetic diversity conserved in genebanks, international research partnerships and farmer participation can help deliver varieties suited to regional growing conditions.
