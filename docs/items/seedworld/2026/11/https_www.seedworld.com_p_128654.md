# Local Expertise, Growing Alongside You

At SeedNet, we are committed to providing farmers with high-quality seed and crop inputs that support success in your fields. Our retail seed farms have hands-on experience with the varieties we offer, so every recommendation is backed by practical knowledge and proven results. With decades of expertise in growing, cleaning, and quality assurance, we deliver high-performing seed designed to help you get the most from every acre. Our exclusive seed lineup features strong varieties for Western Canadian growers. AB Dram Malt Barley , the first non-GN barley bred in Canada, offers CDC Copeland-level yields and early maturity. AAC Galore VB Soft Wheat combines medium height, high yields, and strong resistance to stripe and stem rust. SeedNet also offers two great yellow pea varieties: AAC Planet , with high yields, strong protein potential, and lodging scores, and CDC Citrine , a top performer in 2021 MCVET trials with strong standability, protein potential, and great yields. Explore SeedNet’s full lineup of exclusive, high-performing seed varieties at www.seednet.ca to find the right fit for your fields.
