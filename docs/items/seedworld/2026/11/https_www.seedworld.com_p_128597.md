# Hyperspectral Imaging Could Help Wheat Breeders Select for Resilience

Hyperspectral imaging could help wheat breeders identify promising varieties faster by measuring plant traits that are difficult to assess across large field trials. CSIRO's AcceleTraitPlus project is exploring how drone-based imaging can evaluate photosynthesis, respiration and grain-filling rates to support more efficient breeding decisions.
