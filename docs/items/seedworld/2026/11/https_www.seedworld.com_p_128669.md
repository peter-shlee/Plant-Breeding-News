# Keep Yield Estimates Up to Date with the ABS Production App

Delivering the right seed starts in the field. Every seed grower promises to deliver the right seed, in the right quantity, at the right time. When you’re managing just a few local fields, paper records and spreadsheets may be enough to keep that promise. But as your operation grows, with multiple teams producing crops across different countries and continents, keeping everyone informed becomes much more challenging. A disease report might sit in someone’s inbox for days, while yield estimates still reflect last month’s figures. By the time the information reaches the right people, an opportunity to act may already have passed. To manage production effectively, every team member needs a clear, up-to-date picture of what’s happening in the fields at every stage. Moving beyond handwritten notes and disconnected spreadsheets to a system designed specifically for the seed industry can make all the difference. Get a clear view of your fields and production The ABS Production App is the mobile field-observation tool within ABS Seed, our broader ERP solution. It brings field information together, helping your teams monitor production and keep track of what is happening across your operation. With ABS Seed, you can map your fields and allocate stock seed to each one, whether you’re growing the crop yourself or working with third-party producers. Yield estimates are generated based on the varieties and protocols entered into ABS Seed. Based on your field activity protocols, ABS also schedules inspections and other field activities. The activity calendar gives you an overview of what’s coming up and what’s already overdue. When inspectors visit the fields, they can record observations and take photos directly in the ABS Production App on a tablet. They can also access images of the parent plants, making it easier to compare what they see in the field with the expected characteristics. And because fieldwork doesn’t always happen where there’s an internet connection, the app works both online and offline. Any information entered offline syncs automatically the next time the device connects. Add another perspective with satellite data When inspectors visit the fields, they can record observations and take photos directly in the ABS Production App on a tablet. Field inspections provide valuable information from the ground. Satellite data adds another perspective from above. The app can use satellite data to monitor moisture levels and crop growth across your fields, giving you additional insight into production conditions. Access to this third-party satellite data requires an add-on subscription. Keep yield estimates up to date As new information comes in, yield estimates are updated based on the data entered, whether it comes from field inspectors or satellite observations. These updated estimates are available to every team member through the ABS Seed platform, helping everyone work with the same information when making production decisions. Build stronger relationships with your producers Planning for future seasons is just as important as managing the current one. The ABS Production Module in ABS Seed also allows you to rate your third-party producers on a scale of one to five directly in the app. Over time, this creates a record of producer performance that helps you identify reliable partners for upcoming growing seasons. Delivering on your promises starts with accurate information A seed company’s reputation depends on its ability to deliver what it promises. By bringing field observations, tests and parent images together in one place, the ABS Production App helps you build a more accurate picture of your crops and identify potential problems earlier. A hailstorm, drought, flood or disease outbreak can affect your expected production. When that information is recorded promptly, you can assess the impact and, if a shortage is likely, arrange additional production through another grower. Without accurate and timely reports, you risk discovering a shortage too late to do anything about it. And if you can’t supply the seed you’ve committed to deliver, you may lose more than one order. You could also lose the customer’s trust and future business. See the difference in practice One of our largest clients uses the ABS Production App across its worldwide operations. He tells me the app has changed his life. He now has all his fields, protocols and current activities in one place. He also takes more photos during field inspections and compares them with the standard parent images available in the app. Another advantage is the app’s flexibility. He has created his own lists and protocols in ABS Seed, which are then carried through to the app for each location. It’s a good example of how a system designed around the seed industry can support the way a business actually works, even across multiple locations. Make sure you can deliver what you promise Your customers expect the right seed, in the right quantity, at the right time. Having accurate, up-to-date production information helps you stay ahead of potential problems and make better-informed decisions.
