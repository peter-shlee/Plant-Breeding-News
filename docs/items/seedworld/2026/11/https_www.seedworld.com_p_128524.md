# You’re INVITED: Seed World’s Founding Ambassador Program

Help us connect and strengthen the U.S. seed industry. Great industries are built on great relationships. For decades, Seed World has helped inform, connect, and engage the people and businesses that drive the seed industry forward. Now, we’re taking the next step. We’re building a network of respected industry professionals who share our belief that meaningful conversations, strong relationships, and fresh perspectives are essential to the future of our industry. We’re calling them Seed World Ambassadors. What is a Seed World Ambassador? A Seed World Ambassador is a trusted industry professional who helps us stay connected to the people, ideas, opportunities, and challenges shaping the seed industry. Ambassadors are more than contributors or representatives. They are connectors, conversation starters, and community builders. They help us bring people together, uncover important stories, share valuable perspectives, and create opportunities for meaningful industry engagement. What Does an Ambassador do? Shawn Brook, President, Seed World Group Connect . Introduce us to more of the people we should know, from industry leaders and innovators to emerging voices who deserve more attention. Contribute . Share perspective on emerging industry issues, identify stories worth telling, and help shape conversations that matter. Represent . Participate in industry conversations and events as a visible and trusted voice for Seed World. Engage . Help bring people together through industry events, discussions, interviews, and gatherings. The role is flexible and designed around each Ambassador’s strengths, interests, and professional relationships. We value independent thinking and different points of view. What’s In It For You? Help shape the conversations that shape the industry, bringing attention to the issues, people and ideas you believe deserve a bigger platform. Expand your professional network and connect with influential industry leaders. Extend your influence and share your perspectives with a broader audience. Participate in meaningful conversation about the future of the seed sector. Play a direct role in shaping Seed World’s editorial and industry engagement initiatives. Collaborate with a respected global industry media organization. Founding Ambassadors receive a monthly retainer, support for approved activities, and opportunities to participate in special Seed World initiatives. A Founding Opportunity We’re starting with just three Founding Ambassadors across the United States: three people whose experience, perspective and relationships we believe will make a real difference. Our intention is to create a program that brings real value to the industry, with our Founding Ambassadors helping us decide where to focus and how to grow. The initial commitment is six months, with flexible participation designed to complement your existing professional responsibilities. Let’s build a more connected seed industry. The seed industry’s greatest strength is its people. We believe there’s an opportunity to do more to bring those people together. We’d love to explore what that could look like with you. Interested? Let’s talk.
