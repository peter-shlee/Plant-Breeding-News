# Euroseeds 2026: Key Highlights from Valencia Congress

From October 25–28, Valencia, Spain, welcomed the international seed community for Euroseeds 2026, bringing together industry leaders, policymakers, researchers and innovators to explore the challenges and opportunities shaping the sector. Key discussions focused on New Genomic Techniques (NGTs), Plant Reproductive Material (PRM) reform, plant variety rights and industry resilience. The InnovAction Stage showcased emerging technologies, while the NextGen Forum connected young professionals with experienced industry leaders. Throughout the Congress, collaboration, innovation and strong partnerships emerged as essential to the future of the seed sector. Watch the video above for a look back at the key conversations, innovations and highlights from Euroseeds 2026. Euroseeds 2027 will take place in Paris, France, from October 17–20, 2027.
