# Euroseeds Congress 2026: Connections Beyond the Stage

What makes the Euroseeds Congress a valuable gathering for the international seed industry? Much of the answer lies in the conversations, connections and ideas exchanged beyond the stage. At the Euroseeds Congress 2026 in Valencia, seed sector leaders, innovators, policymakers and businesses from Europe and beyond gathered to explore new technologies, discuss shared challenges and identify opportunities for collaboration. In this video, participants explain what made the experience worthwhile, from meeting key decision-makers and potential business partners to gaining fresh perspectives on the industry’s direction. Organised by Euroseeds, the annual Congress brings the international seed community together to exchange knowledge and strengthen relationships — with a conversation often providing the starting point for something more.
