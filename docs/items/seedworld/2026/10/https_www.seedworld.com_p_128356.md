# 3 Game Changers: InnovAction Winners Announced

Who took home the top prize at the Euroseeds 2026 InnovAction Stage? Ten finalists presented their technologies to an independent jury at the Euroseeds 2026 Congress in Valencia, Spain, showcasing innovations aimed at improving seed performance, crop resilience and sustainability. Three companies emerged as winners: Winner – PEWAS Innovative Chemistry: Named Euroseeds 2026 InnovActor for Aquaholder, a seed coating technology designed to improve water availability during germination. First runner-up – SeedForward: Recognized for GRASSGUARD®, a seed coating technology supporting grass and pasture crop establishment. Second runner-up – Ensemo: Recognized for SeedJection™, a patented technology that introduces beneficial microorganisms directly into seeds.
