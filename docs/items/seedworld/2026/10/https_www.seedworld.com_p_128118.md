# Euroseeds 2026: Global Seed Innovation and Collaboration

The Euroseeds 2026 Congress, taking place October 25–28 in Valencia, Spain, will bring together seed companies, plant breeders and innovators from around the world to discuss the challenges and opportunities shaping the industry’s future. In a conversation with Seed World Group, Garlich von Essen, Secretary General and CEO of Euroseeds, shared his perspectives on the Congress’s expanding international presence, particularly among participants from North and South America, and its importance as a platform for global industry collaboration. The discussion highlighted the growing recognition of plant breeding and seed innovation in addressing broader policy priorities, as well as the emerging technologies creating new opportunities across the sector. Von Essen also discussed the role of the Euroseeds InnovAction Stage in connecting established companies with emerging businesses and technologies, and the importance of engaging the next generation of seed industry leaders. As the global seed sector continues to evolve, the Congress provides an opportunity to exchange ideas, strengthen industry relationships and explore how innovation and collaboration can help shape its future.
