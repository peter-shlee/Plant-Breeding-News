# The Best Seed Coating Service is the One You Don’t Have to Worry About

Our company has been serving the seed industry since 1956, and over the decades we’ve gained a wealth of experience in pretty much everything from packaging and logistics to quality assurance and import-export. This wide-ranging know-how serves our seed coating customers well, ensuring that a sometimes complicated process is efficient, seamless, and timely. Our philosophy is simple: We’re successful when the seed companies we service are successful. We got into the seed coating business because our biggest customer needed that service and had faith in our ability to provide it. To this day, we continue to meet our customer’s needs, no matter what. That means matching our field-proven coatings to the crops they best suit so that our customers can be confident that the product we deliver will perform predictably batch-after-batch and year-over-year. There is no question that there are unique challenges to the seed coating business. I sit down every year with our customers to talk through their needs for the coming season, and yet I do so knowing that, despite our combined best efforts, the schedule needs to include room for the constant fire alarms that will erupt when our busy season rolls around. It’s not at all unusual for seed companies to come to us pretty much every week needing a quick turnaround as they deal with fire alarms of their own. That’s the nature of the business we’re in, and we’ve learned to be prepared, flexible and ready to react quickly with all the necessary adjustments when jobs start arriving in a rush. We have found that strong communication is essential. I tell our customers, if there’s something you guys need, give me a call. If I don’t know the answer, I will get it. Just tell me what seed you’re working with, what you’re trying to achieve, and we will come up with a solution, even if that means developing a customized formulation. Our goal is to consistently produce a product that our customers can be proud to put their brand on, freeing them up to focus their energy on marketing and selling their seeds. One of the biggest compliments we got this year was from a customer that I called to see how a coating and packing job we’d done for them had worked out. He told me that he hadn’t heard anything bad from his operations manager. That may not sound like a glowing report, but our customers tell us that they find our dependability and consistency to be a huge relief. It allows them to focus one hundred per cent on servicing their own customers. They only comment if there’s a problem, and that isn’t often. Businesses need to decide how they measure success, and I know we’re doing a good job if we have consistent year-over-year runs and our customers keep on coming back. They may not talk much, but when they continue to place their trust in us, I know we’re doing well.
