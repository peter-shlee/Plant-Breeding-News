# Study Reveals Lasting Effects on Corn Stress Tolerance

New research involving 47 corn varieties reveals how early-season heat and drought can have lasting effects on plant growth and flowering. The findings highlight genetic differences in corn stress tolerance and could help breeders develop varieties better equipped to withstand multiple environmental stresses.
